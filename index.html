📥 Section 2
Queue Ingestion & Intake Methods
Four pathways for ticket creation and intake — monitor all daily.
📧 2.1 — Email Ingestion (GRC@Tennessee.edu)
▼
  • Forwarding Protocol: Forward incoming requests to GRCSupport@Tennessee.edu to trigger automatic TDX ticket creation.
  • Missing Attachments: If documentation is omitted in the original email, make an explicit note to request files immediately after ticket generation.
📋 2.2 — Direct Review Form Ingestion
▼

Analysts must actively monitor the TDX dashboard report "Open ALL GRC Tickets" daily:

  • Identify items where STATUS = New OR PRIM RESP = Blank / Unassigned
  • Click the Ticket ID in the left column to open ticket details and evaluate form fields
🔄 2.3 — Reassigned TDX Tickets Protocol
▼

When external TDX groups reassign tickets without required intake data:

  1. Advise the requester to submit a formal request via the Risk Review intake portal
  2. Close the current ticket: Click Actions > Update, set Status = Cancelled, and apply template: GRC > New Technology Risk Review Intake Process New Ticket Request
🔀 2.4 — Requester Corrections & Duplicate Merging
▼

Requester Field Updates

If submitted on behalf of another user (e.g., HelpDesk or Contracts), click Edit, update the Requester field to the end user, and click Save.

Merging Duplicate Tickets

Open the ticket to merge, click Actions > Merge Into, copy/paste the primary Ticket ID into the To Ticket field, and save.

1️⃣ Step 1 — Access TDX & Open Ticket
  • Log in to TDX with your UT credentials
  • Navigate to the "Open ALL GRC Tickets" dashboard report
  • Identify tickets with STATUS = New or PRIM RESP = Blank
  • Click Ticket ID to open and review form fields
2️⃣ Step 2 — Set Open & Send Analyst Template
  • Assign yourself as Primary Responsible
  • Change ticket status to Open
  • Apply template: GRC > Analyst Assigned
  • This stops the Respond By SLA clock
  • Start your Time Tracking — Time Started
3️⃣ Step 3 — Request Intake & Security Docs
  • Review all form fields for completeness
  • If missing intake data → apply GRC > New Tech Intake template
  • Request security docs: SOC 2, ISO 27001, HITRUST, or HECVAT
  • Set status to Awaiting Response and pause time
4️⃣ Step 4 — Update Status & Start Time Clock
  • Once requester responds → set status to In Progress
  • Resume time tracking — Time Running
  • Begin UpGuard vendor lookup and evidence review
  • Classify data types and deployment model
5️⃣ Step 5 — 2-Week Lifecycle / Approval Review
  • Resolve By SLA = 14 calendar days from ticket creation
  • Conduct full UpGuard risk assessment
  • Review all evidence for freshness (< 3 years)
  • If score > 600 → proceed to approval
  • If score < 600 → treat/adjust risks and document
6️⃣ Step 6 — Execute Approval / Rejection & Log Time
  • Click Approve Ticket or Reject Ticket in Current Activities panel
  • NEVER set Status = Resolved manually
  • Log all time entries — zero exceptions
  • Document final findings in the TDX feed
  • Time Tracking → Stopped
📊 Section 4
Status, Time-Tracking & SLA Matrix
Every ticket status change dictates a corresponding time-tracking state. Status changes must be recorded immediately.
📊 Ticket Status & Time Tracking Matrix
Ticket Status Operational Definition & Workflow Condition Time Tracking
New Ticket is unassigned in the queue; Respond By SLA clock is actively ticking. Time Not Started
Open Assigned to an analyst; initial triage complete, work preparation underway. Time Started
In Progress Requester responded; analyst is actively conducting risk analysis or review. Time Running
Awaiting Response Waiting on requester or vendor to provide required security documentation. Time Paused
On Hold Paused for authorization, committee review, or set scheduled future date. Time Paused
Solution Proposed Alternative proposal or risk mitigation option presented to requester. Time Paused
Resolved Risk assessment completed and approved/rejected via Current Activities. Time Stopped
⏱ SLA Standards
  • Respond By SLA: Stopped when an analyst assigns responsibility and sends the Analyst Assigned template to the requester.
  • Resolve By SLA: Auto-configured for exactly 14 calendar days (2 weeks) from initial ticket arrival.

💬 Section 6
Communication Etiquette & Feed Management
Ticket communications default to private. Ensure proper visibility settings before sending every comment.
🚨 Public Visibility Alert Warning
If Make comments private remains checked while an external contact is selected in Notify, a red warning banner ("Mark Public") will display. Click Mark Public to grant visibility before saving.
👁 6.1 — Public vs. Private Comment Rules
  • Messaging Requesters: Uncheck Make comments private to make comments visible to external requesters. Select the Requester in the Notify field.
  • Internal Analyst Notes: Leave Make comments private checked to restrict notes to internal OIT users only.
🔽 6.2 — Feed Filtering & Threaded Replies
  • Filtering: Filter ticket feeds using header tabs: All (1), Communications (2), Edits (3), Status Changes (4), and Comments (5)
  • Top Feed Comments: Click the main Comment button to place notes at the top of the feed
  • Threaded Replies: Click the small Comment hyperlink directly under a specific entry to nest responses under parent comments
📅 6.3 — Communication Schedule & Quality Standards
🟢 In Progress Status

Provide status updates every 1 to 2 business days.

🟡 Awaiting Response Status

Follow up every 2 to 3 business days.

✅ Professionalism

Maintain a clear, professional, and courteous tone in all feed entries.

🔍 Section 8
Detailed Risk Assessment Procedures
Subsections 8.1–8.7 covering all risk analysis, handoffs, data classification, and deployment types.
🔎 8.1 — Risk Analysis Protocol
  • Review ticket details, form entries, attachments, and data types
  • Evaluate deployment model, SSO integration, AI functionality, and criticality
  • Inspect UpGuard for existing evidence, vendor summary scores, and prior conditions
  • Document findings and risk decisions (Approval, Remediation, Acceptance) in TDX
🤝 8.2 — Technical Team Handoffs
  • Google Suite: Reassign ticket to OIT_Platform Services
  • Microsoft Integrations: Route to Jet Toomey for permission review
  • Contracts Review: Assign to Stephen Brown, Dan Fast, or Brian Stubblefield
  • Document all handoffs in feed comments and notify the requester
🏷 8.3 — Data Classification & Impact
  • Classify data using actual elements (e.g., GDPR, FERPA, HIPAA, PCI)
  • Apply the highest applicable classification level
  • Verify stated business impact against outage effects
  • Request missing data details before advancing workflow

📋 8.4 — Gap Questionnaires
  • Trigger when evidence coverage is under 90% or missing
  • Assign a strict 2-week (14 calendar day) due date
  • Request SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, HECVAT, or policy files
  • Upload and review vendor responses in UpGuard
📞 8.5 — Vendor Review Meetings
  • Schedule when documentation leaves security questions unanswered
  • Focus on data flow, hosting, incident response, backups, and remediation
  • Log attendees, decisions, action items, and deadlines in TDX feed
📄 8.6 — HECVAT & Assurance Reports
  • Use HECVAT when third-party assurance reports are unavailable
  • Convert Excel HECVAT spreadsheets to PDF prior to UpGuard upload
  • Prioritize Critical and High risks first during evidence validation

🏗 8.7 — Premise & Deployment Classification
☁️ Vendor-Hosted (Cloud)
  • Evaluate vendor data protections
  • Review SOC reports and backup frequency
  • Check subcontractor access
🖥 Self-Hosted (On-Premises)
  • Review internal server controls
  • Check patching schedules
  • Verify access restrictions
🔀 Hybrid Deployment
  • Verify cloud data protections
  • Review on-premises integration interfaces
  • Assess both environments independently
🛡 Section 7
UpGuard Vendor Risk Management SOP
Full step-by-step process for creating, assessing, and managing vendors in UpGuard per UT policy IT1516.
🛡 3-Year Evidence Freshness Rule
SOC 2, ISO 27001, and HITRUST documentation must be dated within the last 3 years. If missing or older, new documentation must be requested.
📊 Risk Score > 600
Triggers full technical risk assessment. Requires detailed vendor profile review and formal risk treatment plan.
🛡 Coverage < 90%
Requires issuing a Gap Questionnaire with a mandatory 2-week (14-day) due date. Upload and review vendor responses in UpGuard.
1️⃣ Access UpGuard & Search/Create Vendor
▼
  • Access UpGuard at https://cyber-risk.upguard.com/ with your UT credentials
  • Navigate to the Vendor Risk tab
  • Search the vendor URL — check if already being monitored
  • If not listed, click "+ Monitor new vendor"
  • Search for vendor — verify domain name matches the correct vendor (many share names)
  • Click "Monitor" — you'll be auto-navigated to the vendor profile editing screen
  • If vendor doesn't appear, click "Attempt to scan [website name]"
  • Navigate to Contacts in the sidebar → click "+ Add contact" with all available vendor contact info
2️⃣ Edit the Vendor Profile
▼

Vendor Summary Box

  • Company Name (update from URL to company name if manually added)
  • Primary Domain
  • Industry (use best judgement or leave blank)
  • Notes — concise overview of vendor's service (use vendor "About us" page if needed)

Custom Attributes Box

  • Software Name
  • Department (requester's dept)
  • Technical Custodian Name & Email
  • Asset Owner Name & Email
  • Deployment Location
  • Critical System Integrations (leave empty if N/A)
  • Other System Integrations (leave empty if N/A)
  • Added by (your name)
  • Date of Approval (added after assessment only)

Labels Box — Relationships

  • In-Use: Vendor is in active use
  • Business Data: Handles private business/enterprise data
  • Customer Data: Handles customer/student private data
  • Network Access: Access to internal networks
  • Physical Access: Physical access to data center or offices

Custom Labels (Compliance)

CGMP · CUI · FERPA · GLBA · HIPAA · In-Review

  • UTC

4️⃣ Complete Security Profile & Upload Evidence
▼
  • Navigate to Security Profile in the sidebar
  • Gather documents from the original ticket or vendor: security policies, audit reports, compliance certificates
  • Click the blue Actions dropdown → + Add Evidence → upload all relevant documents → click Analyze Evidence
  • For ISO 27001: select "ISO 27001:2022" category under Core controls dropdown
  • For SOC 2: select "SOC 2 Compliance" or "SOC 2 Compliance and Attack surface controls" — use whichever outputs the better score

✅ Evidence That Scans Well

  • Full SOC 2 Type II report — complete attestation, not a 1–3 page summary
  • ISO 27001 documentation — prioritize Statement of Applicability (AOC) over simple certificate
  • Completed security questionnaires — HECVAT, UpGuard system questionnaire, policy/evidence packs

❌ Insufficient Evidence (Alone)

  • ISO certificate images or short letters without control scope/detail — use for recordkeeping and expiry tracking only
  • Low-check evidence — leads to low coverage, incomplete narratives, and inflated risk scores
📄 HECVAT → PDF Conversion
Open HECVAT in Excel → File > Save as Adobe PDF → Conversion Range = Entire Workbook → Conversion Options = Fit to paper width → Click 'Convert to PDF'
5️⃣ Domain Selection & Gap Questionnaire
▼
  • While evidence is being analyzed, scroll to Domains and IPs box
  • Click the gear icon → Deselect all domains except the primary domain (unless vendor is a SaaS provider) → Hit "Save changes"
  • After analysis completes, check Check Coverage percentage
  • If coverage is below 90% → click Actions → Request evidence → select "UpGuard Security Profile gap questionnaire" → set vendor contact and due date (2 weeks standard) → Send
  • Ideally wait for vendor response before starting risk assessment. If no response within 2 weeks, proceed with assessment.
6️⃣ Start the Risk Assessment
▼
⚠ Critical Order of Operations
Add and analyze ALL evidence BEFORE starting the risk assessment. If evidence is added after starting, the narrative is point-in-time and won't fully update — you'll need to click Refine to regenerate.
  • Next to Risk assessment, click the "Not started" button
  • AI will conduct the risk assessment — may take a few minutes
  • If risk rating score is 600 or higher → return to Vendor Summary, enter approval date, add "In-Use" tag → approve vendor in TDX → DONE
  • If risk rating is below 600 → continue to risk treatment steps below
7️⃣ Treating & Adjusting Risks (Score Below 600)
▼
  1. Scroll to Manage Risks box → click Filters → select Critical Risk and High Risk under Severity
  2. Review filtered controls — note controls with citations (controls without citations = scanning risk controls)
  3. Go back to Security Profile → search for each noted control → review Summary, Comments, and Citations
❌ Reject Citation

If AI scanned incorrectly (citation has nothing to do with the control/risk). Sometimes auto-clears the risk to "pass."

⚖️ Waive Risk

If citations meet control requirements or risk doesn't auto-clear after rejecting bad citations. Must provide valid justification.

🔧 Adjust Risk

If citations show compensating controls — reduce severity by one level (e.g., High → Medium). Document rationale.

  • After managing all critical/high controls → click your "In Progress" risk assessment → click Refine
  • Select Audience, Level of Detail, Custom Prompt, and Scope using best judgement → click Apply
8️⃣ Complete & Publish Risk Assessment Report
▼
  1. Scroll to Risk details → select all Critical/High risks to highlight → confirm they appear in Key Risks
  2. Optionally edit the executive summary and conclusion using the Pencil icon
  3. Click the blue Publish button → keep the existing reassessment date (may be adjusted later based on criticality)
  4. At top of page → Generate Report → select "Security Profile Risk Assessment Report" → Quick generate
  5. Select "Yes, base this report on a risk assessment" with "Latest – Security Profile Assessment" → Next → Generate
🎉 Congratulations!
You've completed an UpGuard Risk Assessment. Return to TDX and approve/reject the vendor through the Current Activities panel.
